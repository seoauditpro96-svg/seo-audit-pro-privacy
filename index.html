Privacy Policy for SEO Audit Pro

Last updated: October 18, 2026

SEO Audit Pro is a Shopify application operated by Wishlist247. This policy describes how information is processed when merchants install and use the application.

1. Information We Collect

We process Shopify store identifiers, application sessions, product and collection information, SEO metadata, blogs, articles, pages, product image ALT text, saved SEO audit results, and subscription status. Technical logs may also be processed.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to scan store content for SEO issues, generate and save audit reports, provide CSV exports, verify subscriptions, and maintain the application's functionality and security.

3. Store Content

SEO Audit Pro performs read-only scans. It does not automatically modify products, collections, pages, or other store content.

4. Data Storage and Security

Application sessions and audit results are stored using our application infrastructure. We use reasonable security measures to protect stored information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Service Providers

We use Shopify for authorized store access, authentication, and subscription billing, and Render for application hosting and database infrastructure.

6. Retention and Deletion

We retain information as needed to provide the service. When the application is uninstalled, its uninstall handler deletes the store's application sessions and saved audit records from the application database. Information may remain temporarily in operational logs or backups.

7. Customer Information

The application is designed to analyze store content. It does not intentionally retrieve customer profiles, payment card information, or order histories.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell merchant or customer personal information. Information may be shared with service providers as necessary to operate the application or comply with legal requirements.

9. Privacy Requests

Merchants may contact us regarding access, correction, or deletion of information, subject to applicable laws and Shopify's privacy requirements.

10. Subscription Billing

Shopify manages subscription approval and billing. SEO Audit Pro checks subscription status to determine access to paid features. We do not directly collect payment card information.

11. Policy Updates

We may revise this policy as our application or legal obligations change. Updates will appear on this page.
