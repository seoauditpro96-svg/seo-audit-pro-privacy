# Privacy Policy for SEO Audit Pro

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

SEO Audit Pro ("we", "our", or "the App") is a Shopify application operated by Wishlist247. We respect merchant privacy and are committed to protecting information processed through our application.

This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how it is used, how it is stored, and the choices available to merchants.

## 1. Information We Collect

When a merchant installs or uses SEO Audit Pro, we may process the following information:

- Shopify store domain and store identifiers.
- App authentication and session information.
- Product titles, descriptions, SEO titles, and meta descriptions.
- Collection information, blogs, articles, and Shopify pages.
- Product image information and image ALT text.
- SEO scan results, detected issues, and saved audit reports.
- Subscription information and billing status provided by Shopify.
- Technical logs and error information necessary to maintain the App.

SEO Audit Pro does not require a separate user account or password.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Perform SEO audits of supported Shopify store resources.
- Identify missing SEO titles, meta descriptions, content, and image ALT text.
- Generate and display SEO audit reports.
- Save scan results for later review.
- Support CSV report exports.
- Verify subscription eligibility and provide access to paid features.
- Maintain application security, functionality, and reliability.

SEO Audit Pro performs read-only audits. It does not automatically edit or modify merchant store content.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

We use application hosting and database infrastructure to operate SEO Audit Pro and store required application data, including Shopify app sessions and SEO audit results.

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, and misuse.

No method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party providers to deliver our services, including:

- Shopify: Store authentication, authorized API access, and subscription billing.
- Render: Application hosting and database infrastructure.

These providers process information where necessary to operate the App and provide their services.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide SEO auditing services and maintain saved reports.

When SEO Audit Pro is uninstalled, our application processes Shopify's uninstall notification and deletes the store's stored app sessions and SEO audit records from its application database.

Residual information may remain temporarily in infrastructure backups or operational logs in accordance with applicable retention practices.

Merchants may contact us with questions or requests concerning their information.

## 6. Customer Personal Information

SEO Audit Pro is designed to analyze Shopify store content rather than customer records.

The App does not intentionally retrieve customer profiles, payment card information, or order histories to perform SEO audits.

## 7. Information Sharing

We do not sell merchant or customer personal information.

We may share information with service providers when necessary to operate the App, meet legal obligations, or maintain security.

## 8. Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, merchants may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information.

We review privacy requests and respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements and Shopify's privacy processes.

## 9. Subscription and Billing

SEO Audit Pro uses Shopify's subscription billing system.

Subscription approval and billing are handled by Shopify. SEO Audit Pro verifies subscription status to determine access to paid features.

We do not directly collect or store merchant payment card information.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our application, business practices, or legal obligations.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.
